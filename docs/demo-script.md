# Demo script (2 minutes)

Setup before going on stage:

- Phone: logged in as a real player, on the Today tab, screen mirroring or held to the camera.
- Projector: `/tv` open full screen on a laptop.
- Second laptop or phone: `/admin`, signed in, on the finals bet. Keep it off the projector.
- Finals live counter bet exists (O/U 49.5) and the counter shows 0.
- Wifi checked. Hotspot ready.

## Beats

**0:00 to 0:15. Hook.**
Say: "Every hackathon has a favorite for the room. Nobody puts a price on it. Called It does."
Show: /tv on the projector. Point at the QR, the countdown, and the top 8.

**0:15 to 0:35. Join and bet.**
Say: "Scan, pick a name, get 1,000 points."
Phone: Today tab. Show the Drop header and countdown. Tap a bet, pick a side, tap 250, tap Call it.
Projector: the odds on /tv move as the stake lands. Say: "Same backend, no refresh."

**0:35 to 0:55. The market.**
Say: "Odds are the share of the pot. The long shot pays the most."
Phone: the stake sheet payout line. Point at the streak flame. Say: "Bet every Drop for +10% per Drop, up to 2x."

**0:55 to 1:15. Reveal.**
Phone: open the reveal story from the finished Drop. Tap through two cards. Point at the CALLED tiles and the "who called it" chips.
Say: "Like Wordle, but it is your friends' money."

**1:15 to 1:35. Groups and AI drafts.**
Phone: Me tab, open a group. Say: "Private groups write their own bets, the creator settles them."
Admin screen: paste notes into AI drafts, show six drafted bets, tap approve on one. Say: "The host pastes a transcript, the model drafts the bets, the host stays in control."

**1:35 to 2:00. Punchline: the agent counter.**
Say: "Finalists are about to demo. Over or under 49.5 times someone says 'agent'."
Projector: the live counter at 0. Admin: tap +1 live, once, as you say the word. The number ticks on the projector.
Say: "That is a bet on this room, settling live. Scan the QR. You are in for Drop 5."

## What to click, in order

1. Projector: /tv.
2. Phone: Today, a bet, stake sheet, Call it.
3. Phone: Board tab, then reveal.
4. Phone: Me tab, group.
5. Admin: AI drafts, then the live counter +1.

## Backup plan if wifi fails

- Switch the laptop and phone to a phone hotspot first. The app only needs internet, not the venue network.
- If the live site is down, play the screen recording (TODO parent: record a 60 second backup video and keep it on the laptop desktop).
- If the phone will not load, show the Revyl cloud iPhone session in the browser. It runs the same build.
- If nothing works, walk through docs/screenshots in the README on the laptop and tell the counter punchline out loud.
- Keep one offline copy of this script and the README screenshots.
