# Demo script (2 minutes)

Setup before going on stage:

- Phone: signed in as one player of a demo group, on the Groups screen. Mirrored or on the camera.
- Second phone or laptop, not on the projector: signed in as the friend the bet will be about (Jill in our demo group).
- Laptop: `/admin` open and unlocked, on the demo replay panel. Keep it off the projector.
- Real 5:00 PM results are saved as a snapshot in the replay panel. Check it exists.
- Wifi checked. Phone hotspot ready.

## Beats

**0:00 to 0:15. Hook.**
Say: "Your friends already bet on each other in the group chat. We gave it odds."
Show: the Groups screen. Point at total points, best rank and nemesis.

**0:15 to 0:40. The chat.**
Tap into the group. Say: "Bets are messages. This one is from a friend, this one is from the AI, and it says so."
Point at the three formats: Yes/No, Over/Under, This or That. Point at the emoji reactions on a bet: "Those are the people in, like tapbacks."

**0:40 to 1:00. Write a bet.**
Tap +. Pick the friend, pick a format, type a question in the compose bar, pick a side and 100 points, send.
Optional: tap "Ideas from your chats" and pick one. Say: "On a real phone, Apple's on-device model drafts these from chats you choose to share. Only the bet text leaves the phone. Today the suggestions are staged."
Watch the odds move when a second phone stakes.

**1:00 to 1:20. Invisible ink.**
Switch to the second phone (Jill). Show the bet about her under invisible ink, with only her name visible, and the line "Unlocks at 5:00 · N friends are in".
Say: "She knows people are betting on her. She does not know on what."

**1:20 to 1:45. The reveal.**
On Jill's phone, rub the ink. The question and everyone's positions appear. Tap what happened. Say: "Honor system. She makes the call, or she can veto and everyone is refunded."
Payouts post in the chat. Point at the winner's +points message.

**1:45 to 2:00. Leaderboard.**
Open group details. Show the leaderboard and the dinner pot. Say: "Last place buys the first round. Called It."

## What to click, in order

1. Phone: Groups, open the group.
2. Phone: +, write a bet, send.
3. Second phone: bet about Jill under ink.
4. Second phone: rub, call it.
5. Phone: chat payouts, then group details.

## Backup plan

- If the reveal is not live yet or the real results are gone: open `/admin`, demo replay panel. Tap "Replay: reveal in 60s" (or "Replay: reveal now"). It saves a snapshot, resets the drop, and sets the reveal time. Run the reveal beat on stage.
- Afterwards, tap Restore next to the snapshot named "Before replay" to put the real 5 PM results back.
- If wifi fails: switch phone and laptop to the hotspot. The app only needs internet.
- If the live site is down: show the screenshots in the README and tell the story out loud. TODO (parent): record a 60 second screen recording as a last resort.
- If the AI draft button fails: use the staged suggestion cards. They need no model call.
