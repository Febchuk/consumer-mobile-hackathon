Called It

Called It lives in iMessage.

It is a private prediction game for invited groups of friends, played inside the Messages app on iPhone.

How to get it

  1. You need an iPhone with iMessage turned on.
  2. Called It is an invited beta. Ask the friend who invited you for the TestFlight invitation; this page has no download link.
  3. Install the app, sign in with your phone number, then go back to Messages and tap the card you were sent.

On Android, or reading this from a text message?

For now Called It works only on iPhone with iMessage. There is no Android app and no web version, and nothing can be viewed or picked from this page.

This page does not sign you in and shows nothing about any game. Called It does not read your chats, photos or contacts.
